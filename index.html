01THE MARKET PROBLEM

The asset may be real.
The signal is still scattered.

An IM, PDF files, a financial model, broker notes, images, emails and conversations. Before an opportunity can be presented with confidence, someone has to bring the evidence together, check every figure and decide what the other side is allowed to see.

The problem is not a lack of information. It is a lack of structure, traceability and fit.
